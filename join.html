Get Involved

There are several ways of getting involved. Whether you volunteer your time, your knowledge or your skills, we welcome and appreciate your involvement. It is through participation from members of our community that we are able to share our community’s deep and meaningful heritage.

Three ways to take part

$30 a year

Become a member

Household and lifetime memberships. Members receive The Mountain ReView quarterly, invitations to every event, and discounts on fee-based events.

Join or renew

No experience needed

Volunteer

Walking tour docent, History Center docent, community outreach, board service, educational programmes, event staffing and communications.

See opportunities

Tax-deductible

Donate

Financial gifts fund preservation, free lectures, publications and interpretive plaques. We also accept donations of items for the archive.

Make a donation

Why it matters

Everything here is done by volunteers

The Association has no paid staff. Every walking tour, every newsletter, every plaque and every hour the History Center is open exists because someone in Mountain View decided to give their time to it.

If you have an hour a month or a day a week, there is something useful you can do — and you will learn a great deal about your own city in the process.

Mountain View Historical Association

Founded in 1954, the MVHA is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Mountain View, California.

MVHA is a registered 501(c)(3). EIN 94-6115407.
