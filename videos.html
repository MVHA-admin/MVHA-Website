Video Gallery

Recordings of our events and lectures on the history of Mountain View. Subscribe to our YouTube channel for new uploads.

MVHA YouTube channel

Recorded talks and webinars

Pacific Press & Shoreline West

Spring 2022 — the history of Mountain View’s first major industrial operation and the historic neighbourhood around it, now known as Shoreline West.

A Path to History: The Story of Stevens Creek

Winter 2022 — the history of Stevens Creek and the Stevens Creek Trail, with MVHA president Pamela Baird, Friends of Stevens Creek Trail president Greg Unangst, and founder Rhonda Farrar.

Rengstorff House: 30 Years of Beauty, Transformed!

Spring 2021 — the Rengstorff House, Shoreline Park and the North Bayshore district, with the Friends of “R” House, the city’s Shoreline Division, and a former resident who grew up in the house in the 1960s.

Votes for MV Women!

Fall 2020 — how women in the Mountain View area mobilised to help secure the right to vote, marking the centennial of the 19th Amendment. With Dr Amy Ellison of the Los Altos History Museum.

Castro Street: Past, Present, Future

Summer 2020 — from dirt roadway and plank sidewalks in the 1860s, to four lanes of traffic and neon in the 1940s, to the 1990 revamp and the pandemic-era pedestrianisation. With Nick Perry and Gehl designer John Bela.

50th Anniversary of “Silicon Valley”

Winter 2021 — the past, present and future of Mountain View’s East Whisman area and its role in the early history of Silicon Valley. With David Laws (Computer History Museum), Nikki Lowy (Google) and Brooke Ray Smith (Lendlease).

Mountain View Historical Association

Founded in 1954, the MVHA is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Mountain View, California.

MVHA is a registered 501(c)(3). EIN 94-6115407.
