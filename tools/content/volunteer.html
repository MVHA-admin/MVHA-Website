Volunteer with the MVHA

The MVHA offers a wide variety of ways to volunteer and help share Mountain View’s history with the public. No experience is required — we will train you.

Where you could help

Volunteer opportunities

Tell us which of these interest you and one of our current volunteers will get in touch with more information.

Walking tour docent

Get trained to give guided walking tours of historic downtown Mountain View.

History Center docent

Help our History Center librarian share Mountain View history with visitors at the library.

Community outreach

Greet the public at the MVHA’s table during local events — neighbourhood ice cream socials, festivals and more.

Board of Directors

Be part of the team that plans future MVHA events and projects.

Educational programmes

Help give presentations on Mountain View history to local schools and organisations.

Event staffing

Assist with set-up and staffing at our quarterly public events at the historic Adobe Building.

Communications

Newsletter editing, social media posting support, and writing newsletter content.
