Mountain View History Center

The History Center’s mission is to collect, organise and make available Mountain View historical resources, and to promote the city’s heritage.

An archive you can visit

The History Center was founded in partnership with the City of Mountain View Public Library around 1967. It holds the historic archives of both the Mountain View Historical Association and the Mountain View Public Library.

The Association’s archives include an irreplaceable collection of scrapbooks, yearbooks, diaries, photographs, maps and other small artifacts of local significance. Historical books and pamphlets on the history of Mountain View are also available for purchase at the History Center.
