Useful Links

Known throughout the world as the home of Silicon Valley, Mountain View and its neighbouring Santa Clara Valley cities are closely associated with technologies shaping our future. But history has a home here too, and the MVHA is one of many organisations working to preserve the multi-layered history of the Santa Clara Valley.

Mountain View history

  • Calisphere: Mountain View History Center photo archive

    Many of the historical photographs in the History Center’s archives have been scanned and are accessible online through the University of California’s Calisphere website.

  • City of Mountain View history page

    A short but informative section on Mountain View history from the city’s own website.

  • Friends of the Rengstorff House

    A non-profit group dedicated to the preservation of the oldest home still standing in Mountain View, at Shoreline Park.

  • Moffett Field Museum

    A museum dedicated to the rich history of Mountain View’s former naval air base.
