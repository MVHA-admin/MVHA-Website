In person

What is not on the website

Far more sits on the shelves than has been digitised: scrapbooks, yearbooks, diaries, maps, business records and the complete paper run of the newsletter. The Mountain View History Center holds all of it.

A volunteer librarian is there on Tuesday afternoons and will help you find what you are after. There is no charge, and you do not need to be a member.

Visit the History Center Ask us a question

Researching a house or a family?

Start with the city directories to place a name at an address, then look for the street or the building in the photographs. The timeline will tell you what else was happening in the city that year.

If you find something we do not have, we would be glad to hear about it.
