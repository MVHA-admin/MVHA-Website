MVHS Eagles Soar

A monument and historic plaque at Eagle Park, dedicated to the accomplishment of the 1975 Mountain View High School football team.

The Mountain View High School Eagles soared again on 16 December 2023, when a new monument and historic plaque was dedicated to the accomplishment of the 1975 football team. Against the odds, the small team won the CCS football championship. Attended by team members and supporters, the unveiling ceremony drew over one hundred observers.

Alberto Olmos was the featured speaker. Pamela Baird served as emcee. Mark Perry gave a spirited retelling of life at Mountain View High School when it was located on Castro Street. Susan Metcalf planned the ceremony — including baking cookies in the shape of footballs.

Nearly fifty years in the making

In 2015 the football team held a forty-year reunion in downtown Mountain View. It was at that reunion that the idea of a monument was first discussed. The intention was to honour not only the 1975 CCS championship team, but the entire MVHS experience.

Several team members approached the Mountain View Historical Association to ask for help organising, publicising and raising funds for the monument. Alberto Olmos worked with the Parks and Recreation Department and the PRC Commission to gain permission and approval to place a monument in Eagle Park. He researched artists and foundries to locate the eagle sculpture, which stands on a metal framework above the plaque explaining the team, the school and the accomplishment.

Almost fifty years later, Mountain View High School alumni and the Mountain View Historical Association, with the help of the City of Mountain View, collaborated to place this monument close to High School Way. Two dedicated benches face the monument and what used to be the scoreboard on the north end of the football field.

The next time you visit Eagle Park, take a look at the monument. It is a great accolade for the team whose motto was “You gotta believe”.
