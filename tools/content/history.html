History of Mountain View

Shaped by many different cultures and eras — and still changing.

The history of Mountain View is shaped by many different cultures and eras. From the native Ohlone peoples who have lived here for millennia, to the Mexican-era Castro family who owned the vast Rancho Pastoria de las Borregas, to the Gold Rush-era pioneers who founded the town near a stagecoach stop on El Camino Real, to the waves of immigrants from around the globe who created the bountiful agricultural landscape of the “Valley of Heart’s Delight” — and then, the technological innovations of Silicon Valley.

This section of the site highlights just some of the many milestones and stories that make up Mountain View’s rich history.
