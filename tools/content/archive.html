Where these come from

Held by the Internet Archive, not by us

Most of the photographs above were digitised through California Revealed, a State Library programme, and are kept permanently by the Internet Archive. This website shows the Archive’s pictures and links to them; it holds no copies of its own. That is deliberate — it means the photographs outlive any website, and that the record you reach is always the fullest one, with the original catalogue entry beside it.

Browse the collections in full

Both collections can be browsed and searched on the Internet Archive itself, where each photograph carries its complete catalogue record and can be downloaded at full size.
