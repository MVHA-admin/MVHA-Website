Who we are

A volunteer association, seventy years on

On 9 September 1954, a small group of long-time Mountain View residents gathered to discuss the future of their city’s past. New development was changing the face of the city, and they realised much of their hometown’s history risked being lost.

Seven decades later, the Association still runs on volunteers. Our goals are to:

  • Gather and preserve historical data on the early settlement and development of the area in and around Mountain View
  • Collect, record, edit and publish a history of the pioneering activities of this area
  • Preserve historic objects and places
  • Cooperate with other organisations in the Santa Clara Valley on projects related to regional and Mountain View history
