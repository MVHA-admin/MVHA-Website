Donate

Thanks to the generosity of our community, the Association has preserved priceless artifacts, provided free historical lectures, published a comprehensive history of Mountain View, and installed interpretive plaques and signs throughout the city.

Ways to give

Option one

Give online

The quickest way to support our work. Every gift goes directly to preservation, programmes and the archive.

Option two

Give by post

Option three

Add to our archive

Donate photographs, documents or artifacts of local historical significance. Fill in the item donation form and email it to us.

Named fund

Castro Street MVHS monument

To donate specifically to the Castro Street MVHS monument, make your check payable to The Mountain View Pioneer & Historical Association and write MVHS monument in the memo line.

Mail to: Mountain View Historical Association, P.O. Box 252, Mountain View, CA 94042

About the monument

Mountain View Historical Association

Founded in 1954, the MVHA is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Mountain View, California.

MVHA is a registered 501(c)(3). EIN 94-6115407.
