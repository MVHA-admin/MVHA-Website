Events & Walking Tours

Our quarterly membership meetings are open to the general public and typically fall on the first Sunday of February, May, August and November at the historic Adobe Building. Guided walking tours of historic downtown Mountain View run through the year.

Mountain View Historical Association

Founded in 1954, the MVHA is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Mountain View, California.

MVHA is a registered 501(c)(3). EIN 94-6115407.
