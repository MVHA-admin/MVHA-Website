Recommended reading

Three books about Mountain View

All three are held by the Mountain View Public Library, and some are for sale at the History Center. We do not sell them online.

2002

Milestones: A History of Mountain View

By Mary Jo Ignoffo. The standard single-volume history of the city, written by a local historian and published for the centennial of incorporation.

Find it at the library →

2006

Images of America: Mountain View

By Nicholas Perry. A photographic history in the Arcadia series, drawing on many of the same collections as the photographs on this site.

Find it at the library →

2012

Then & Now: Mountain View

By Nicholas Perry. Historical views set beside photographs taken from the same spot today — the clearest way to see how much of the city has changed.

Find it at the library →
