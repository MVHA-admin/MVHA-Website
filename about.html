About the Association

Founded in 1954, the Mountain View Historical Association is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Mountain View, California.

Our goals

What the Association sets out to do

  • Gather and preserve historical data relative to the early settlement and development of the area in and around the present City of Mountain View
  • Collect, record, edit and publish a history of the pioneering activities of this area
  • Preserve historic objects and places
  • Cooperate with other organisations in the Santa Clara Valley on projects related to regional and Mountain View history

We are a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit, EIN 94-6115407. Everyone you see on this page is a volunteer.

Open to everyone

Quarterly membership meetings

The MVHA holds quarterly general membership meetings and events, typically on the first Sunday of February, May, August and November in the afternoon at the historic Adobe Building, 157 Moffett Boulevard, Mountain View.

Quarterly meetings are free to members and open to the general public.

Board meetings

The MVHA Board of Directors generally meets on the first Wednesday of every month at 6:00 p.m., with no board meetings in January, April, July or October. Most meetings are held over Zoom.

Interested and prospective members are welcome to attend.

The Board of Directors

The MVHA is governed by an 11-member Board of Directors elected or appointed every two years. President, Vice President, Treasurer and Secretary are elected by members at the Association’s annual fall meeting in even-numbered years. Along with the most recent Past President, these five serve as the Executive Committee. The rest of the board is made up of appointed standing committee chairs — Historical Data, Membership, Newsletter, Ways & Means — two directors-at-large, and non-voting chairs of special committees.

Interested in a board position?

The Association welcomes individuals interested in board positions to get in touch and learn more about volunteering. Email info@mountainviewhistorical.org or leave a voicemail at (650) 903-6890.
